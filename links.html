  • This site is a calendar detailing upcoming chess-related events in both Washington and Oregon.

National or International links

Below are a couple links that are the official websites of chess organizations.

  • USCF
  • This site is the official website of the United States Chess Federation, which serves as the USA's chess governing body. As the holder of this role, it is also the USA's official representative for FIDE. It administers the USA's official chess rating system, which is similar to the system used by NWSRS.
  • CFC (FCE)
  • This site is the official website of the Chess Federation of Canada (Fédération Canadienne des Échecs), which serves as the national chess organization of Canada. Like the USCF, it administers ratings and organizes tournaments.
  • FIDE
  • This site is the official website of the International Chess Federation. Located in Switzerland, it is the governing body of international chess competition that connects the national federations.

Playig Sites

Below are a few popular online playing sites.

  • Chess.com
  • Currently the most popular chessplaying site going, with over one million chess games played daily. They also operate chesskid.com as a safe website for elementary chessplayers.
  • Lichess
  • The best free chess playing site, and almost as popular as chess.com. Clean interface, no registration required, no ads, no plugin required. Play chess with the computer, friends, or random opponents. Great free "Learn Chess Basics" and "Practice" sections to improve your game.
  • The Internet Chess Club
  • A pay site ($24.50 per year for students) with 24 hour games in a friendly environment. Play chess, siamese (bughouse), giveaway, and other fun chess variants too. Consistent and reliable ratings. Parents: this site has filters for rude behavior.
